Design that communicates with purpose.

Hi, I'm Alfin Firmansyah — a digital designer with interests in UI/UX, visual identity, logo and packaging design, front-end development, and game development. I use Figma to shape clear interfaces and bring digital experiences to life. I enjoy turning ideas into thoughtful, polished work, with close attention to clarity and detail. My goal is for every project I take on to have a humanitarian purpose and benefit people and communities, especially through work in education and healthcare. I'm also interested in how business and digital marketing can help meaningful ideas reach the people they're intended to serve.
